# School portal user guide

This guide explains how to use the school portal, step by step. It is for everyone who uses it:

- the platform operator, who runs the service for many schools;
- school staff: the school admin, the principal, the registrar, the accountant, the counselor, teachers and the gate scanner;
- students and parents.

Words in **bold** are the exact words you see on the screen: menu names, tabs, buttons and fields.

## 1. What the app does

The school portal keeps a school's records in one place:

- students, their guardians and their classes;
- staff, classes and subjects;
- daily attendance, with ID cards that are scanned at the gate;
- marks, published results and printed report cards;
- school fees, payments and receipts;
- alerts and announcements to staff, students and parents.

Each person signs in and sees only the menu for their role.

### Who uses it

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Platform operator | Creates schools and their first admin. Turns features such as Fees on or off. Sets the price list, bills schools and records their payments. Resets any user's password. |
| School admin | Runs the whole school: students, staff, classes, attendance, grades, fees, messages and school settings. Creates staff accounts. Manages users and passwords. Sees the school's bill from the platform. |
| Principal (academic admin) | Sets up the school year, classes, sections, subjects, grade levels, timetables and teacher assignments. Views and admits students, and adds teachers. Runs promotion and graduation. Publishes results and signs report cards. Sees the school's attendance (read only). Has a Fees menu to review fees and approve voids, discounts, report card holds and currency resets. |
| Registrar | Views, admits, imports and places students. Manages classes and sections. Sees the school's attendance (read only). Does not set up the school year, promote or graduate. |
| Accountant | Fee setup, issuing invoices, recording payments, reports and expenses. Can see the student list. |
| Financial admin | Everything the accountant does, plus discounts, voiding older payments, resetting a student's currency and releasing held report cards. Can see the student list. |
| Counselor | Can look at the student list, student profiles and students' attendance, and read messages. |
| Teacher | Marks attendance for the class they sponsor, enters marks for the subjects they teach, and sends announcements to their own classes. |
| Gate scanner | Scans student ID cards at the gate. Sees nothing else. |
| Student | Sees their timetable, courses, published results, attendance and messages. |
| Parent | Sees each child's attendance, published results, fees and receipts, and messages. Can report an absence. |

In the app, the principal's role is called **Academic admins** (for example in the **Users** list). This guide calls it "principal".

## 2. Getting started

### 2.1 The addresses

The app runs at `https://app.<your-domain>`. Replace `<your-domain>` with the platform's real web address once it is set up.

- **Each school has its own address:** `https://app.<your-domain>/<school>`. For example, `https://app.<your-domain>/top-foundation`.
- **Each school has its own sign-in page:** `https://app.<your-domain>/<school>/login`. The page shows the school's logo and name.
- **The platform operator signs in at** `https://app.<your-domain>/platform/login`.

Always use your school's address. Ask the school office for the right link.

The plain address `https://app.<your-domain>` opens **Find your school**. Type your school's web address or code (the name after the slash in your school's link, for example `top-foundation`), then tap **Go to sign in**. On a phone you used before, it opens your school's sign-in page by itself.

Tip: put the school's sign-in link on a notice, or share it on WhatsApp, so that parents can save it.

### 2.2 Signing in

1. Open your school's sign-in page.
2. Under **Email or username**, type your email address or your username.
3. Under **Password**, type your password.
4. Tap **Sign in**.

The app opens your home page.

Good to know:

- You can sign in with your email or your username. You cannot sign in with a phone number.
- People without an email address get a username, such as `ama.kollie`. It is printed on the sign-in slip from the school office.
- The school office creates all accounts. The sign-in page says: "Your school office creates accounts: ask them to set one up for you."

### 2.3 Your first sign-in

The school office gives you a password when your account is made. That password is for the first sign-in only.

1. Sign in with the username and password from the school office.
2. The app opens **Change your password**. You can't use any other page until you finish this step.
3. In **Current password**, type the password the office gave you.
4. In **New password**, type a new password. It must have at least 8 characters, with upper and lower case letters, a number and a symbol.
5. Type it again in **Confirm new password**.
6. Tap **Change password**.

The app says "Password changed. Opening your dashboard…" and opens your home page.

Keep your password secret. On a shared phone, always sign out when you finish (see 2.6).

### 2.4 Forgot your password

**If your account has an email address:**

1. On the sign-in page, tap **Forgot password?**.
2. Type your email in **Email address**.
3. Tap **Send reset link**.
4. Open the email and tap the link.
5. On **Reset your password**, type a new password twice.
6. Tap **Reset password**, then sign in.

**If your account has no email address:** the app says "No email? Ask your school office to reset your password."

1. Go to the school office.
2. The office opens **Users** (in the user menu), finds you and taps **Reset password**.
3. The office gives you the new password. It is shown only once.
4. Sign in with it. The app then asks you to choose your own password.

School staff who forget their password follow the same steps. The platform operator can also reset the password of any school user.

### 2.5 Too many wrong passwords

After 5 wrong passwords, the account is locked for 15 minutes. The app says "Too many login attempts. Please wait 15 minutes and try again."

You have two choices:

- Wait 15 minutes, then try again.
- Ask the school office to reset your password. A reset also removes the lock.

### 2.6 Using the app on a phone

The app works in the web browser of any smartphone. You do not need to install anything.

- **The menu** is a bar at the bottom of the screen. When a role has more than five menu items, the last button is **More**, which opens the rest.
- **The pages of a menu item** appear as tabs across the top. Slide them sideways to see more.
- **The bell** at the top right is your inbox. A red number shows how many messages you haven't read.
- **Your name or initials** at the top right open the user menu. It has **Your profile**, **Change password**, **Notification settings** and **Sign out**. Staff who sign report cards also see **My signature**.
- **Your profile** changes your picture and phone number. Staff can also change their name there. Students and parents can't: their name, gender, date of birth and class are kept by the school office (**Edit details** on the student's profile).
- **Shared phone:** when you finish, open the user menu and tap **Sign out**. Then the next person can't see your child's details.
- **Saving data:** on a slow connection, wait for each page to finish loading before you tap again.

On a computer, the menu is a list down the left side instead of a bar at the bottom.

## 3. Setting up a new school, end to end

Follow these steps in order. Each step says who does it, where to find it in the menu, and what to check before going on.

### Step 1. The operator creates the school and its first admin

**Who:** the platform operator. **Where:** **Schools**.

1. Sign in at `/platform/login`.
2. Open **Schools**, then tap **Create school**.
3. On step 1 of 3, **School details**, fill in:
   - **School name**;
   - **Code** (letters and numbers only);
   - **School address**: the short name after the slash in the school's link, for example `top-foundation`. Use only lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. The form shows the full address under the field;
   - optionally the logo (**Choose logo file**) and the **Primary color**.
4. Tap **Next: admin setup**.
5. On step 2 of 3, **Admin account**, enter the first admin's **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Email Address**. Leave **Generate secure password automatically (Recommended)** ticked.
6. Tap **Create school and admin**.
7. Step 3 of 3, **Sign-in details**, shows the **Login URL**, the **Username/Email** and the password. Copy them, or tap **Download Credentials (Secure File)**.
   - These details are shown only once and hide after 5 minutes.
8. Tick the box that you have copied the details, then tap **Finish school setup**.
9. Give the details to the school admin in person or by a private message. Delete any downloaded file afterwards.

**Check:** the school is in the **Schools** list as **Active**. Open the **Login URL**: it should show the school's name.

### Step 2. The operator chooses the school's areas

**Who:** the platform operator. **Where:** **Schools**.

The areas are what a school can use. Students, classes, the school year, grades and staff are always on. These can be switched off:

| Area | What it covers | New schools |
|---|---|---|
| **Fees** | Fee setup, invoices, payments, receipts, expenses, the families' fee pages | Off |
| **Attendance** | Daily attendance, scanning, excuses, attendance reports, ID cards | On |
| **Homework** | Assignments teachers set and students hand in | On |
| **Timetable** | Class timetables | On |
| **Announcements** | Announcements to staff, students and families (the inbox and school alerts stay on) | On |
| **SMS alerts** | Text-message copies of alerts | Off (not connected yet) |

1. In the **Schools** list, find the school and tap **Areas**.
2. Tick the areas the school will use. Tick **Fees** if it will use the app for school fees.
3. Tap **Save Changes**.

**Check:** the school's menus show only the areas that are on. An area that is off is refused for everyone at the school, admins included: its pages say "(Area) is turned off", and on **Roles** and **Role assignment** its permissions and roles are greyed out with a lock.

### Step 3. The operator enters the price list and payment details

**Who:** the platform operator. **Where:** **Billing → Price list**. This is done once for the whole platform, not once per school.

1. Open **Billing → Price list** and tap **New price list**.
2. Fill in **Starts on**, and the USD and LRD amounts for:
   - **Staff, per month**;
   - **Parent or student, per month**;
   - **Term minimum**.
3. Tap **Add price list**.
4. Under **How schools pay**, type the bank account and mobile money details that schools should use.
5. Tap **Save payment details**.

**Check:** **In force today** shows the price list. Without a price list, no invoice can be issued. A new school's first term is free.

### Step 4. The admin signs in and completes the school profile

**Who:** the school admin. **Where:** the user menu (tap your name at the top right).

1. Sign in with the details from the operator, and choose your own password.
2. **Name and colours:** open **School settings**, then **Tenant Settings**. Check the name and the colours, then save.
3. **School profile:** open **School profile** from the user menu.
   - Fill in **Address**, **Motto**, **Phone** and **Email**. These are printed on report cards.
   - Under **Who signs as principal**, choose the principal. Under **Title under the signature**, type the title, for example "Principal".
   - Tap **Save school profile**.
4. **School logo:** on the same page, under **School logo**, tap **Choose a photo or scan**. Use a square image, PNG or JPEG, up to 1 MB. Tap **Save logo**. The logo shows on the sign-in page, at the top of the app, on receipts and on report cards.
   - A logo can also be given as a web link in **School settings** (**Logo URL (optional)**). A link saved there replaces the uploaded logo.
5. **School seal:** under **School seal**, tap **Choose a photo or scan**. Use a clear photo of the stamp on white paper, PNG or JPEG, up to 1 MB. Tap **Save seal**.
6. **Signatures:** each person who signs report cards adds their own. The principal and every class sponsor open **My signature** from the user menu (see 4.2).

**Check:** **School profile** shows the logo, the principal's name and the seal.

### Step 5. The admin sets up the school year with the wizard

**Who:** the school admin or the principal. **Where:** **Classes → Setup**. The registrar has no **Setup** tab.

The wizard has five steps. It saves as you go, so you can stop and come back later.

1. Tap **Create a school year**.
   - Type the **Name** (for example 2026/2027), the **First day** and the **Last day**.
   - Tick **Make this the current school year**.
   - Tap **Create school year**.
2. **If the school used the app last year:** tap **Choose what to copy**, tick what you want, and tap **Copy from** (last year). Nothing already there is overwritten.
3. **Step 1, Terms:** tap **Add 2 semesters × 3 periods**. Check the dates and use **Change dates** where needed.
4. **Step 2, Levels and sections:**
   - Tap **Add a grade level** for each grade (for example "Grade 1"), with its **Order**.
   - For each level, tap **Add section** (A, B, …).
5. **Step 3, Classes and sponsors:**
   - Enter the **Seats in each class** (30 unless you change it), then tap **Create N classes**. This makes one class for each level and section. Change one class's seats later under **Classes → Classes** (Edit).
   - Choose a sponsor for each class. If a teacher is missing, use **Add a teacher**.
6. **Step 4, Subjects and teachers:**
   - For each level, tap **Choose subjects**, tick the subjects, and tap **Apply to N classes**.
   - Then choose a teacher for each class subject.
   - Subjects you need must exist first. Add them under **Classes → Subjects** (**Add Subject**).
7. **Step 5, Enroll students:** admit or import the students into these classes (see Step 7 below). Teachers, sponsors and subjects can come before or after.

**Check:** the top of the page says **All 5 steps done**. Tap **See summary**: it lists what the year has and the next steps (grading, fees and ID cards).

You can also manage these items one at a time under the other **Classes** tabs: **Classes**, **Sections**, **Subjects**, **Grade levels**, **Years and terms** and **Semesters**.

### Step 6. Add teachers and staff

**Who:** the school admin or the principal. **Where:** **Staff → Add teacher**.

1. Fill in **First name** and **Last name**. Everything else is optional.
2. Tap **Add teacher**.
3. The app shows **Sign-in details**: the **Username** and **Password**. Copy them or write them down now, because the password is not shown again.
4. Give the details to the teacher.
5. Assign subjects in the wizard (Step 4) or under **Staff → Subject assignments**.

**Other staff** (principal, registrar, accountant, financial admin, counselor, gate scanner): the school admin adds them.

1. Open **Users** from the user menu and tap **Add staff member**.
2. Fill in **First name** and **Last name**, and choose the **Role**.
   - **Email**, **Username** and **Phone number** are optional. Without a username, the app makes one from the name, like `ama.kollie`.
   - A school can't create an admin. Another admin comes from the platform operator.
3. Tap **Create account**.
4. The app shows the username and password once. Copy them, or tap **Print sign-in slips**, and hand them over. The person chooses a new password at their first sign-in.

To change a person's roles later, use **Role assignment** in the user menu: tick or untick the roles and tap **Save roles**. The rules:

- You can't change your own roles.
- Only the platform operator gives or removes the **admin** role.
- You can only give roles that allow nothing more than your own roles do.
- A role whose area is turned off for the school is greyed out with a lock.

**Roles** in the user menu shows what each role allows, read only, grouped by area (Students, Fees, Attendance and so on) in plain words. The technical name is in small print underneath. Roles show their everyday names too: **Principal** for `academic-admin`, **Gate scanner** for `attendance-scanner`, **Accountant (bursar)** for `accountant`. Roles and what they allow are set by the platform operator. If someone needs access that no role gives, ask the operator.

**Check:** under **Staff → Directory**, the **Sponsor of** column shows each sponsor's class.

### Step 7. Admit or import students, with their guardians

**Who:** the school admin, the principal or the registrar. **Where:** **Students → Admit** or **Students → Import**.

**One student at a time:**

1. Open **Students → Admit**.
2. Under **Student**, fill in **First name**, **Last name** and **Gender**. Leave **Admission number** empty to get the next number.
3. Under **Guardian**, choose one:
   - **Find an existing guardian** (for brothers and sisters, who share one guardian);
   - **Add a new guardian**: name, **Relationship to the student** and **Phone number**;
   - **No guardian yet**.
4. Under **Class**, choose the student's class.
5. Tap **Admit student**.
6. The app shows the username and password of the student and of any new guardian. They are shown only once. Tap **Print sign-in slips** and hand the slips over.

Guardians sign in with their username or email. The phone number is for school messages and calls.

**Many students from a spreadsheet:**

1. Open **Students → Import** and tap **Download template**.
2. Fill in one student per row, with their class (for example "Grade 1 - A") and guardian.
3. Tap **Choose a spreadsheet** and pick the file (Excel or CSV, up to 500 rows).
4. Check that each column is matched. If the whole file is one class, leave the class column empty and choose the class under **Class for rows without one**. Then tap **Check rows**. Fix any row marked with a problem, and upload again.
5. When it says **All rows are ready**, tap **Import N students**.
6. Tap **Print sign-in slips** or **Download as CSV**. The passwords are shown only once.

Admitting or importing a student also enrolls them in the class you chose. There is nothing more to do for them in **Class placement**.

**Check:** **Students → Directory** lists the students with their class, and **Students → Enrollments** lists them under that year, grade and section.

### Step 8. Place students in classes

**Who:** the school admin, the principal or the registrar. **Where:** **Students → Class placement**.

Students admitted or imported with a class are already placed (and enrolled). Use this page only for anyone who is not placed yet, or to move a student to another class.

1. Open the **Not placed** tab.
2. Tick the students. A bar appears at the bottom.
3. Choose the class in **Place N students in**, then tap **Place students**.

To move one student, find them on the **Placed** tab, tap **Move**, choose the class, and tap **Move to** (class).

**Check:** the **Not placed** tab says **All placed**.

### Step 9. Grading settings

**Who:** the school admin or the principal. **Where:** **Grades → Grading settings**.

1. **Pass mark:** type the school's pass mark in **Pass mark (0 to 100)** and tap **Save pass mark**. The default is 70.
2. **Categories and weights:** under **Grading Schemas**, tap **Create Schema**.
   - Give it a **Schema Name** and choose the **Academic Year**.
   - Add each category with its **Weight (%)**, for example Tests 40, Assignments 30, Quizzes 20, Participation 10.
   - The weights must add up to exactly 100%.
   - Tap **Create Schema**.

**Check:** in **Grades → Gradebook**, when a teacher adds an assessment, the **Category** list shows these categories.

### Step 10. Fee setup and Issue invoices

**Who:** the school admin, the accountant or the financial admin. **Where:** **Fees → Fee setup**. This only shows when the operator has turned on Fees (Step 2).

1. **Categories** tab: tap **Add category** for each kind of fee, for example Tuition, Registration and Uniform.
2. **Fees** tab: choose the **School year** and **Semester**.
   - Tap **Add fee**.
   - Choose the **Grade level** (or **All grade levels**) and the **Category**.
   - Type the **Amount in USD**, the **Amount in LRD**, or both.
   - Tap **Add fee**. Repeat for each fee.
3. **Installment plan** tab:
   - Add the due dates and the **Share of the bill (%)** of each installment, for example 50% by 15 September and 50% by 15 November. The shares must add up to 100%.
   - Tap **Save installment plan**.
4. Tap **Issue invoices**, choose the **Semester**, and tap **Issue invoices**. This bills every student enrolled this year.
5. Read the result. If it says "needs a look", add the missing fee and issue invoices again. Running it again only adds what is missing.

Students admitted after the invoices were issued are billed automatically.

**Check:** **Fees → Student accounts** shows each student with a balance.

### Step 11. Attendance settings and ID cards

**Who:** the school admin. **Where:** the user menu, then **Attendance rules**, and **Attendance → ID cards**.

1. Open **Attendance rules** from the user menu and check:
   - **School timezone** (Africa/Monrovia);
   - **School days**;
   - **Late after**, **Scanning closes** and **Auto-absent at**;
   - **Check-out scanning**.
2. Tap **Save settings**.
3. Under **Holidays & make-up days**, add each holiday with its **Date** and **Label**, then tap **Add**.
4. Open **Attendance → ID cards**, choose a class, and tap **Print cards**. Repeat for each class.
5. Cut out the cards and give them to the students.
6. Give the gate scanner an account: **Users → Add staff member**, role **Gate scanner** (see Step 6 and 4.8).

**Check:** scan one card at the gate. It should show the student's name in green.

### Step 12. First announcement

**Who:** the school admin. **Where:** **Messages → Announcements**.

1. Tap **New announcement**.
2. Under **Send to**, choose **Everyone**.
3. Type a **Title** and a **Message**, for example to welcome families and explain how to sign in.
4. Tap **Next**, check it, and tap **Send announcement**.

**Check:** the announcement appears in the list with "sent to N people".

## 4. Guide per role

Each section lists the role's menu, then their usual tasks, then what to do when something goes wrong.

Everyone also has:

- the **bell** (inbox);
- the user menu, with **Your profile**, **Change password**, **Notification settings** and **Sign out**.

### 4.1 Platform operator

**Your menu:**

| Menu | Tabs |
|---|---|
| Home | Overview, Analytics |
| Schools | Schools |
| Billing | Overview, Invoices, Price list |
| Users | Users, Roles and permissions |
| Audit | Security and audit |
| System | Health, API docs |

**Sign in** at `/platform/login` with **Email or username** and **Password**, then tap **Sign In to Console**. School accounts can't sign in here.

#### Add a new school

See Steps 1 to 3 in section 3.

#### Turn an area on or off for a school

1. Open **Schools**.
2. Find the school and tap **Areas**.
3. Tick or untick the areas (see Step 2 in section 3).
4. Tap **Save Changes**.

#### Switch a school off or on

In **Schools**, tap **Deactivate** or **Activate** on the school's row, then confirm. Use **Delete** only for a school that was created by mistake: it can't be undone.

#### Create a staff account for a school

Schools add their own staff under **Users → Add staff member** (section 3, Step 6). Use this when a school needs another **admin** (schools can't create admins), or when it has no admin who can sign in.

1. Open **Users** and tap **Create User**.
2. Fill in **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Email Address**.
3. Choose the school under **School**, and the role under **Assigned Role**.
4. Choose **Auto-generate secure password**.
5. Tap **Create Account**. The password appears once, in the message at the corner of the screen. Copy it straight away.

#### Change what a role allows

Only you create roles and change what they allow. Schools see their roles read only, and ask you when someone needs more.

**For one school only** (for example, School A's accountant should also see student records):

1. Open **Users → Roles and permissions**.
2. Under **School**, choose the school.
3. Choose the role and tick or untick its permissions. They are grouped by area, with plain names.
4. Tap **Save for** (school). Every other school keeps the role as it is. The role shows **Adjusted for** (school), and on the school's **Roles** page it says "Set up for your school by the platform operator".
5. To undo, tap **Reset to built-in**.

**For every school:** leave **School** on **Every school (built-in roles)**, change the role, and tap **Save for every school**. A school that has its own version keeps the permissions you adjusted for it; the rest follow the change.

Whatever a role allows, a school never gets an area you turned off for it (section 3, Step 2).

#### Reset a school user's password

1. Open **Users**.
2. Search for the person by name, email or username.
3. Tap the **Reset Password** button on their row, then **Reset password**.
4. The window **New password for** (name) shows the username and the new password. Tap **Copy all** and pass them on. They are not shown again.
5. The person is signed out everywhere, and must choose a new password at their next sign-in.

#### Billing: how it works

- There is one price list for all schools.
- **Staff** are billed every month of the term while their account is active.
- **Parents and students** are billed only for the months they signed in.
- There is one invoice per term (semester). The app issues it by itself at the start of each term.
- A new school's first term is free.
- An invoice is due 30 days after it is issued.
- The school admins get reminders on the due date, and 7 and 14 days after it.
- At **30 days overdue**, the school becomes **read-only**: people can sign in, look and print, but can't change anything.
- At **60 days overdue**, the school is **suspended**: only the school's admins can sign in, to pay and to export data.
- Paying brings access back at once.

#### Weekly: check what schools owe

1. Open **Billing → Overview**.
2. Look at the tiles **Billed**, **Collected**, **Outstanding** and **Not paid yet**, and the count of schools in each status.
3. Under **Schools**, filter by **Status** to find schools that are **Read-only** or **Suspended**.

#### Record a school's payment

1. Open **Billing → Overview** and tap the school.
2. Under **Invoices**, find the invoice and tap **Record payment**.
3. Choose the **Currency**. The first payment fixes the invoice's currency.
4. Fill in the **Amount**, the **Method** (**Bank**, **Mobile money** or **Cash**), the **Reference** and **Received on**.
5. Tap **Record payment**, then **Open receipt** to print it.

Online payment is not available yet.

#### Other billing tasks (on the school's billing page)

- **Issue an invoice now:** tap **Issue invoice**, choose the **Term**, and confirm.
- **Give a school more time:** open the menu, tap **Give more time**, choose a date in **Grace until**, and tap **Set grace**. Until that date, the school won't become read-only or suspended.
- **A school that has stopped for now:** tap **Mark dormant**. It pays nothing and is read-only. Its data is kept for a year. To undo, tap **Make active again**.
- **A mistake:** use **Void invoice** or **Void** on a payment, and give a reason. An invoice that has payments can't be voided: void its payments first.

#### The payment details schools see

Open **Billing → Price list**. Edit the text under **How schools pay** and tap **Save payment details**. Schools see this text on their **Billing** page.

#### When something goes wrong

- **"No price list is in force yet":** add a price list that starts today or earlier.
- **"This school has no terms yet":** the school must create its school year and semesters first (section 3, Step 5).
- **A school admin is locked out or forgot their password:** reset it under **Users**.

#### Health and audit

- **System → Health** shows measured figures only: **Database size**, **Open database connections**, **Database response** time and **API running for** (uptime).
- **Audit → Security and audit:** under **Audit Logs**, tap **Export CSV** to download the latest logs of the type you chose.

### 4.2 School admin

**Your menu:**

| Menu | Tabs |
|---|---|
| Home | Home |
| Students | Directory, Admit, Import, Enrollments, Class placement, Promotion, Graduation, Remedial |
| Staff | Directory, Add teacher, Subject assignments |
| Classes | Classes, Class rosters, Sections, Subjects, Grade levels, Years and terms, Semesters, Timetable, Setup |
| Attendance | Today, Mark, Scan, Excuses, Reports, ID cards |
| Grades | Gradebook, Review and publish, Report cards, Assignments, Grading settings, Remedial |
| Fees | Overview, Student accounts, Payments, Fee setup, Reports, Expenses, Settings (only when Fees is on) |
| Messages | Announcements, Inbox |

A student's attendance record opens from **Attendance → Reports** or from the student's profile. Holidays are set in **Attendance rules** (user menu).

**Your user menu** also has:

- **My signature**;
- **School settings**, **School profile**, **Users**, **Roles** and **Role assignment**;
- **Attendance rules**, **Delivery log**, **Audit log** and **Billing**.

On a phone, the bottom bar shows **Home**, **Students**, **Staff**, **Classes** and **More**.

#### Daily

1. **Attendance → Today:** see who is present, late, absent or not marked, class by class. Tap a class to open its roster.
2. **Excuses** (if the Today page says excuses are waiting):

## 5. The school year cycle

A school year has 2 semesters. Each semester has 3 periods and one exam:

- **1st semester:** periods 1st, 2nd and 3rd, then the semester exam.
- **2nd semester:** periods 4th, 5th and 6th, then the semester exam.

### 5.1 How grades are worked out

- **Period score:** the average of each category's assessments, weighted as set in **Grading settings**. Categories with no assessment in that period are left out.
- **Semester average** = (period 1 + period 2 + period 3 + semester exam) ÷ 4. If a mark is missing, the average uses only the marks that exist and is marked incomplete.
- **Yearly average** = the average of the two semester averages.
- **Pass mark:** each school sets its own (70 unless changed). A subject is passed when its yearly average is at or above the pass mark.

### 5.2 Start of the year

| Task | Who | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Create the year, terms, levels, sections, classes, sponsors, subjects and teachers | Admin or principal | **Classes → Setup** |
| Admit and import new students; place everyone in a class | Admin, principal or registrar | **Students → Admit**, **Import**, **Class placement** |
| Check the pass mark and categories | Admin or principal | **Grades → Grading settings** |
| Check school days, times and holidays | Admin | **Attendance rules** (user menu) |
| Print ID cards for new students and new classes | Admin | **Attendance → ID cards** |
| Set the fees for semester 1 and issue invoices | Admin, accountant or financial admin | **Fees → Fee setup** |
| Send a welcome announcement | Admin | **Messages → Announcements** |

### 5.3 Each period

1. **Teachers** add assessments and enter marks in **Gradebook**, then save.
2. **The principal** checks **Grades → Review and publish**. When every subject is complete, they tap **Publish results** for each class.
3. **Students and parents** get a "Results published" alert and can see the results.
4. **The principal or admin** prints report cards in **Grades → Report cards**, if the school gives paper cards each period.

### 5.4 Each semester

1. After periods 1–3 are published, teachers enter the semester exam (choose "Semester 1 exam" in **Gradebook**).
2. The principal publishes "Semester 1 exam and average" for each class.
3. The principal or admin prints report cards. Each card shows the semester average, rank and a QR code.
4. **Before the next semester starts**, the accountant:
   - opens **Fees → Fee setup** and chooses the new semester;
   - checks the fees and installment plan;
   - taps **Issue invoices**.
5. The platform issues the school's own term invoice. The school admin sees it under **Billing**.

### 5.5 End of the year

1. **Publish everything.** All six periods and both semester exams must be published for every class.
2. **Print final report cards.**
3. **Create the new year:**
   - Open **Classes → Setup** and tap **Create a school year**. Its semesters and periods are added for you.
   - Leave **Make this the current school year** unticked until graduation is done.
   - Use **Choose what to copy** to copy last year's classes, sponsors and subjects.
4. **Promotion**, one grade at a time (school admin or principal):
   - Open **Students → Promotion**.
   - Choose the **Academic Year** (the year that is ending), **Promote into** (the new year) and the **Grade**, and tap **Evaluate Eligibility**.
   - Check the results. Each student is **Eligible**, **Repeating**, **Conditional** or **Not graded**.
   - Tap **Execute Transition**, then **Confirm & Execute**. Eligible students move up one grade into the new year. Repeating and Conditional students repeat their grade: they go into the same grade's class in the new year (their own section if it has a class, otherwise the class with the most free places) and get remedial sessions. Not graded students are skipped: grade them and run it again, or place them by hand.
   - A Conditional student who later passes remedial work is moved up by hand: open **Students → Class placement**, tap **Move** and choose the class of the next grade.
   - Repeat for each grade. Running it again for the same grade is safe.
   - The final grade is not moved: those students graduate in the next step.
5. **Graduation:**
   - Open **Students → Graduation**. It lists the students in the final grade of the school year shown. Choose the ending year if another year is shown.
   - Students marked eligible are ticked. Check the list.
   - Tap **Graduate N students**, then **Graduate students**.
6. **Make the new year current:** open **Classes → Years and terms** and tap **Activate** on the new year.
7. **Finish the new year's setup** in **Classes → Setup**:
   - check each step;
   - promoted and repeating students are already in their new classes;
   - admit the new students.
8. **Fees:** set the new year's fees and issue invoices. Each student's currency is chosen again by their first payment of the new year.
9. **Receipts:** receipt numbers start again at 00001 for the new year, for example 2027-00001. The number uses the year the school year starts in.

Promotion and graduation change many records at once. Do them on a computer, one grade at a time, and check the results before you continue.

## 6. Troubleshooting and questions

### 6.1 I can't sign in

1. **Check the address.** Use your school's own page: `https://app.<your-domain>/<school>/login`.
2. **Check the username.** Use your email or your username, not your phone number. The username is on your sign-in slip.
3. **Check the password.** Passwords care about capital letters. Check that the keyboard is not in capitals.
4. **"Incorrect username or password":** try again carefully, or use **Forgot password?** (only if your account has an email address).
5. **Still no luck:** ask the school office to reset your password. The school admin does it under **Users** in the user menu.

The platform operator can reset a school admin's password.

### 6.2 I'm locked out

After 5 wrong passwords, the app says "Too many login attempts. Please wait 15 minutes and try again."

- Wait 15 minutes, or
- ask the school office for a password reset, which removes the lock at once.

**"Can't sign in" with a message about the school's account:** the school is suspended for an unpaid platform bill. See 6.8.

### 6.3 A payment was taken in the wrong currency

The first payment of the year fixes a student's currency. To change it:

1. The person who took the payment voids it the same day (**Fees → Payments → Void payment**). After that day, the principal, the financial admin or the school admin voids it.
2. The principal, the financial admin or the school admin opens the student's statement and taps **Reset currency**. This only works when none of this year's payments still count.
3. Record the payment again in the right currency.

### 6.4 A payment was entered by mistake

- **Same day, by the person who took it:** open **Fees → Payments**, find the receipt, tap **Void payment**, and give a reason.
- **Later, or someone else's payment:** ask the principal, the financial admin or the school admin to void it.

A voided receipt is never deleted. It stays on the list marked VOID, and the student's balance goes back up. Parents don't get an alert for a void.

### 6.5 A student is in the wrong class

1. Open **Students → Class placement**.
2. Find the student on the **Placed** tab and tap **Move**.
3. Choose the right class and tap **Move to** (class).

If you need a new ID card for the student, open their profile, then **ID card → Issue a new card**.

### 6.6 A parent can't see results

Check these in order:

1. **Are the results published?** Open **Grades → Review and publish**, choose the period, and look at the class. If it says **Not published**, the principal must publish it.
2. **Is the report card held for fees?** The parent and the student see "Report card on hold", with the reason.
   - In **Fees → Settings**, check whether **Hold report cards when fees are overdue** is on.
   - The family can pay the overdue amount, or the principal, the financial admin or the school admin can tap **Release report cards** on the student's statement.
3. **Is the parent linked to the child?** If the parent sees "No children are linked to your account", add them as a guardian on the student's **Guardians** tab.

### 6.7 The gate scanner is offline

- Keep scanning. Scans are saved on the device and sent when the connection is back.
- Keep the scanner page open until the message "N scans waiting to send" disappears. Tap **Send now** to try at once.
- Scans saved on an earlier day don't count. Remove them with **Remove them**, and let class sponsors correct those days in **Attendance → My class**.
- Without a connection for a long time, class sponsors can mark attendance by hand instead.

### 6.8 The school account is read-only or suspended

A banner at the top of the screen shows the school's billing status.

| Status | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Due soon | The platform invoice is due within 7 days. Only admins see this. | Pay before the due date. |
| Overdue | The invoice is past its due date. | Pay soon. The account becomes read-only at 30 days overdue. |
| Read-only | 30 days overdue. Everyone can sign in, view and print, but nothing can be changed. | The school admin pays the amount shown. |
| Suspended | 60 days overdue. Only school admins can sign in. | The school admin pays, and can still export data. |
| Dormant | Set by the platform operator. View only. | Contact the platform operator. |

To pay:

1. The school admin opens **Billing** from the user menu.
2. Read **How to pay** and pay by bank or mobile money, giving the invoice number.
3. The platform operator records the payment, and access comes back at once.

Staff who are not admins see "Ask the school office."

A school's data is never deleted for non-payment. A school admin can always use **Download export**.

### 6.9 Other questions

**Can parents pay online?**
Not yet. Online payment is not available yet. Families pay at the school or by bank or mobile money, and the school records the payment.

**Can parents get SMS messages?**
Not yet. SMS is not available yet. Alerts go to the in-app inbox, and by email to people with an email address.

**Can parents reply to a message?**
Not yet. Two-way messages are not available yet.

**Can teachers write remarks on report cards?**
Not yet. Report card remarks are not available yet. The remarks lines print blank, to fill in by hand.

**How do I check that a printed report card is real?**
Scan its QR code with a phone camera. Or go to `https://app.<your-domain>/verify` and type the code printed under the QR code. The page says **Valid report card**, **Superseded** (a newer card exists) or "We couldn't find this report card".

**A student lost their ID card.**
Open the student's profile, then **ID card → Issue a new card**, then **Print card**. The old card stops working at the gate.

**I don't want alert emails.**
Open **Notification settings** from the user menu and switch off the alerts you don't want by email. They still arrive in your inbox.

**Why are some pages in a different style?**
Some older pages, such as **Enrollments**, **Promotion**, **Remedial**, **Subject assignments** and most **Classes** tabs, still use an older design. They work, but their words may differ a little from this guide.

## 7. Glossary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Academic admin | The app's name for the principal's role. |
| Admission number | The student's school number. It is printed on the ID card and can be typed at the gate. |
| Assessment | A test, quiz, assignment, project or exam that gets a mark. |
| Category | A group of assessments with a weight, for example Tests 40%. |
| Class | One grade level and section in one school year, for example "Grade 5 - A". |
| Class sponsor | The teacher in charge of a class. Marks its attendance and signs its report cards. |
| Current school year | The year the app uses for classes, attendance, marks and fees. |
| Cut-off | The time after which students not yet marked are recorded absent. |
| Dormant | A school paused by the platform: free and view only. |
| Excused | An absence with an approved reason. |
| Grade level | A year group, for example Grade 5. |
| Guardian | A parent or other adult who follows a student in the app. |
| Inbox | The list of alerts and announcements behind the bell. |
| Installment | A part of a semester bill with its own due date. |
| Invoice | A bill. Students get one per semester; schools get one per term from the platform. |
| LRD | Liberian dollars. |
| Pass mark | The lowest yearly average that passes a subject. Each school sets it. |
| Period | One of six marking periods in the year, three per semester. |
| Platform operator | The person or company that runs the app for all schools. |
| Publish | Make a class's results visible to its students and parents. |
| Read-only | The school can view and print, but can't change anything, because of an unpaid bill. |
| Receipt | Proof of a payment, numbered per school year, for example 2026-00001. |
| Section | A group within a grade level, for example A or B. |
| Semester | Half of the school year: three periods and an exam. |
| Suspended | Only school admins can sign in, because of a bill 60 days overdue. |
| Username | A short sign-in name, for example ama.kollie, for people without an email address. |
| USD | US dollars. |
| Void | Cancel a payment or invoice. It stays on record, marked VOID. |
